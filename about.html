About South Carolina Electoral History

Understanding our past helps build a better future.

Our Purpose

This website was created to educate educators and Students alike and show them our state's history and trend using our electoral history and major events in SC history.

A History of Change

From the War of Independence to the Civil Rights Movement and Modern Day, South Carolina has been on the forefront for national issues and change on a political Scale. Our state can show our nations global change on different issues.

What You'll Find here

  • Interactive Maps showing results from different elections
  • Information about major candidates and parties
  • A Timeline of Major events in SC History
  • Opportunities to share your thoughts and Feedback with us!

Sources & Accuracy

Using our team of expert SC Historians using Official government resources, research, and archives, we give the best account and overview of events to make sure we show you the most accurate information on historical topics, making sure you always get the most accurate information on any topic.
